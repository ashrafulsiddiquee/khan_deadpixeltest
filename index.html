What is a dead pixel test?

A dead pixel test fills your whole screen with one solid color at a time so faulty pixels stand out. It works on any monitor, laptop, TV, tablet or phone with a browser, including LCD, LED, IPS, VA and OLED panels. Use it when buying a new or used display, after a delivery, or when you notice a spot on your screen.

Dead pixel vs stuck pixel

Dead pixel

A pixel that stays black because it gets no power. Check it on a white screen.

Stuck pixel

A pixel that is always lit in one color: red, green, blue or white. Check it on a black screen. Stuck pixels sometimes recover.

Subpixel defect

A single red, green or blue part of a pixel is faulty. It shows up only on certain colors, so test red, green and blue separately.

How to test your monitor

Clean the screen first, because dust looks like a faulty pixel. Set the brightness to maximum, press Start full test, and go through black, white, red, green, blue, cyan, magenta and yellow. Look at every corner and edge. Use the gray steps to spot uneven backlight and clouding on dark screens.

Can I fix a stuck pixel?

Dead pixels are usually permanent. A stuck pixel may come back by cycling colors for several minutes, or by gently pressing the spot with a soft cloth while the screen is on. Never press hard, since that can damage the panel. If the display is under warranty, contact the seller or manufacturer first, because many brands accept returns only above a certain number of faulty pixels.

Gradient and banding test

The gradient patterns show smooth ramps from black to white and across red, green and blue. Visible stripes or steps mean banding, which can come from a low-quality panel, poor color settings or limited color depth. The color bars and grid help you check color order and screen geometry.

Backlight bleed and uniformity test

Run the near-black and gray patterns in a dark room with the lights off. Bright patches at the edges or corners indicate backlight bleed. Blotchy areas on a gray screen indicate uneven backlight or clouding. A little is normal on many LCD monitors.

Stuck pixel fixer

The fixer opens a small window that flashes red, green, blue, white and black. Drag it over the stuck pixel and let it run for 10 minutes or more. Results vary, and it does not work on dead pixels. Do not use it if flashing lights affect you.

Frequently asked questions

Is this test safe for my screen?

Yes. It only shows solid colors and does not stress the panel. On OLED screens, avoid leaving one bright color on for a long time.

Does it work on phones and TVs?

Yes. Open this page in the phone or TV browser and tap through the colors.

Does it upload or store anything?

No. The test runs entirely in your browser.
